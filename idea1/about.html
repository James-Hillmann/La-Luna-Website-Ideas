About La Luna

Our story, our mission, and the team behind the magic.

Our Mission

More than gymnastics —
a community

At La Luna Rhythmic Gymnastics Academy, we believe every child deserves the chance to discover their potential. Founded in Sammamish, WA, we've built a welcoming, professional environment where young athletes from age 4 to 18 develop strength, artistry, discipline, and joy.

Our certified coaches bring international experience and genuine passion to every class — from beginner fundamentals to competitive training at the highest levels.

4,000+ Students trained
10+ Years in Sammamish
1,000+ 5-star reviews

What we stand for

Our Values

01

Safety First

Every class is designed with age-appropriate progressions and certified coaching to ensure your child trains safely and confidently.

02

Inclusive Community

We welcome students of all backgrounds and ability levels. Everyone belongs at La Luna — from first-timers to competitive athletes.

03

Excellence in Coaching

Our coaches hold international certifications and bring decades of experience at the regional, national, and world level.

04

Joy of Movement

Above all, we cultivate a genuine love for gymnastics — because athletes who love what they do achieve the most.
